About MCWM

Mercury Capital & Wealth Management

Philosophy

Markets generate more data than any one person can track. MCWM's products exist to turn that data into structured, interpretable market intelligence — not signals to blindly follow, but context to make your own decisions with. Research first, opinions second.

Why APEX Straddle

Index-options traders need to see volatility, flow and regime together, in real time, without stitching together five tools. APEX Straddle is that single surface for NIFTY/SENSEX straddle analytics.

Research, data & decision support

Data is measured, never guessed — a value the platform cannot verify is labelled as such, not defaulted. Analytics are derived transparently from that data. Decision-support scores (confidence, regime, conviction) always carry their sample size, so you can judge how much weight they deserve. The decision itself stays yours — see Risk Disclosure.

Market Intelligence

Expected Move, VRP, Skew, OI Flow, Regime — what they mean and how to read them

Expected Move — the range the market is currently pricing in for the remaining session/expiry, derived from option premiums. VRP (Variance Risk Premium) — gap between implied and realised volatility; a context signal, not a trade trigger. Skew — relative pricing of CE vs PE at equal distance from ATM; read it as sentiment context, not a standalone entry signal. OI Intelligence — Open Interest build-up/unwind classified across strikes to flag where positioning is concentrated. Market Regime — a data-driven classification of the current volatility/trend state, always shown with its own confidence and sample size — never a plain percentage with no basis. None of these are buy/sell signals; they are inputs to your own read of the market.

What the data-state labels mean

LIVE / FRESH / STALE / UNAVAILABLE / ERROR — read before you trust a number

LIVE — market is open and this value is updating in real time. FRESH — recently updated, within the expected freshness window for this data type. STALE — older than that window; treat with caution, the underlying feed may have paused. UNAVAILABLE — the provider is not currently returning this data at all; nothing is shown in its place, nothing is guessed. ERROR — the last fetch/save for this item failed; retry or check System Health below. These states describe the data, not a trade signal — interpret what you see, do not blindly act on it.
